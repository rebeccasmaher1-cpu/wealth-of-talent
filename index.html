Principles

What makes it work.

Centralised Function

One P&C team serves all ten businesses. Consistency, reduced cost, institutional knowledge.

Proposal-Based

Every engagement starts with a scoped proposal. Leaders know exactly what they're getting.

E-Signature via Ignition

Proposals e-signed through Ignition. Invoices on Xero. Full audit trail.

Scalable Capacity

One hire or a full workforce build-out — P&C scales to match demand.

Group-Wide Visibility

MDs and Heads of see workforce planning and HR initiatives across the group.

Leaders Stay Focused

P&C handles end-to-end so leaders focus on running their business.

Billing

Two ways to engage.

Depending on the nature of the work.

Model A

Time & Materials

Billed on hours worked plus direct costs at cost. Best for ongoing recruitment, advisory, and HR services where scope may evolve.

Model B

Project-Based

Fixed scope, defined deliverable, timeline, and cost. Best for compliance audits, frameworks, and cultural strategy.
